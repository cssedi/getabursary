MTN Chartered Accountant (CA) Bursary Programme

MTN Group

The MTN CA Programme is a SAICA-accredited Chartered Accountant training programme based at MTN Group's financial head offices - distinct from the broader MTN Foundation undergraduate bursary, this programme places trainee accountants directly into MTN.

Amount
Market-related salary + study leave + payment of APT and QE2 exams

Eligible Fields of Study

MTN Group supports students in the following disciplines

Chartered Accountancy (CA)
BCom Accounting (Honours)
Certificate in the Theory of Accounting (CTA)

Eligibility Requirements

  • ✓ Completed BComAcc (Hons) or CTA, OR currently in the final year of BComAcc (Hons) / CTA
  • ✓ South African citizen
  • ✓ Strong academic record
  • ✓ Commitment to completing the SAICA training programme
  • ✓ Available for full-time training at MTN Group's financial head offices
  • ✓ Application submitted before 31 May closing date

What's Included

  • ✓ Market-related salary throughout training
  • ✓ Generous study leave for SAICA exam preparation
  • ✓ Full payment of APT (Advanced Programme in Tax) exam fees

Work Commitment

MTN CA trainees complete a 3-year SAICA-accredited training contract at MTN Group with rotation across financial functions. There is typically no additional work-back beyond the training contract.
